Operations pane: select shapes in the Geometry pane (Ctrl/Cmd/Shift+click to add or remove one), then add a profile cut with your default tool through the stock. Operations are listed in cutting order and can be selected, renamed (double-click or F2), enabled or disabled, moved up or down and deleted; each shows whether its toolpath is computing, failed or has warnings. While an operation is selected, the Properties panel edits its name, tool (picked from your library and snapshotted into the project), shapes and profile settings (side, direction, depth, allowance, finish pass, lead-in arc and ramp), with lengths in the project's units. Every edit is one undo step.
