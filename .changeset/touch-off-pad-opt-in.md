Importing a file no longer adds a touch-off pad: tick **Touch-off pad** in the zero settings, and it is placed on the XY zero. The pad can have its own tool-change G-code, suggested from the machine's with its move put on the pad, and the viewport marks it with a dashed ring and a "Z touch-off" label instead of a disc that looked like the bit.
