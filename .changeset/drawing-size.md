Clicking a drawing's shape now also shows the drawing's properties over the viewport, where you can set its width or height (it keeps its proportions), fit it to the stock with a border, or remove it. Profile tabs placed on its paths move with it. A new drawing now lands its border in from the zero's anchor. The selected drawing is shaded in the viewport, and selected paths are drawn wider with a faint outline so they stand out on the stock.
