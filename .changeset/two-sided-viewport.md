Projects can be made two-sided in the project properties, choosing the axis the stock is turned over about and the parting depth. A Top / Bottom switch in the viewport shows each side, and the bottom side's simulation starts from the stock the top side leaves, turned over. Operations show their side, and new ones go on the side in view.
