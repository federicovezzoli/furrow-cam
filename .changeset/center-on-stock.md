A drawing's or model's properties now have a *Center on stock* button that moves it to the middle of the stock in X and Y, whatever the zero point; a model keeps its height.

The properties panes keep their header in view while scrolling, a model's Remove button is red, and the model pane no longer scrolls sideways in longer languages.
