Drill operation. A drill operation's params are a `depth` (of the tool tip below the stock top) and `peck`. Holes are single-point paths and closed paths a circle fits within 0.1 mm on the diameter (every point of the outline within 0.05 mm of one radius), so circles exported as polygons or Béziers, like OpenSCAD's 36-gons, count while coarse polygons don't; a hole's size is the circle through its farthest points. The tool plunges into points and the circles it fits: straight to depth at its plunge rate, or with pecking in equal pecks no deeper than its step-down, retracting to just above the stock after each. An end mill bores larger circles with climb helices no steeper than its plunge angle (along the chords) and no more than its step-down per turn, each ending with a full turn at depth, in rings from the inside out no further apart than its step-over when the hole is over twice its diameter; a ring shorter than the tool radius is plunged a step-down at a time with a turn at each depth instead; a drill bit skips them, and circles smaller than the tool are skipped, with a warning. Holes are ordered to shorten travel: nearest neighbour from the hole nearest the stock's bottom-left corner, then 2-opt. Drills and end mills can drill, V-bits can't (`canDrill`). In the Operations pane, "Add drill" creates one with the default tool (or the first that can drill), through the stock and pecking; the Properties panel edits its depth and pecking. `@furrow/cam-core` adds `drillToolpath`, `drillOrder`, `DRILL_DIAMETER_TOLERANCE` and `arcStep`.
