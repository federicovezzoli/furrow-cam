A profile's start point can have a plunge point: right after placing it on a closed path, click beside the part or on a pre-drilled hole. The tool then goes up and down only there and feeds straight to the start, so T-slot and dovetail cutters can cut profiles. Plunge points can't be used together with holding tabs.
